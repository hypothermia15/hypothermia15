# Miraç Barış Usta

**Child and Adolescent Psychiatrist · Associate Professor**\
Department of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry, Ondokuz Mayıs University Faculty of Medicine, Samsun, Türkiye

I combine clinical work with research. I see children, adolescents and their families at Ondokuz Mayıs University, supervise residents and medical students, and study how artificial intelligence can support the assessment of neurodevelopmental disorders and the training of clinicians.

## Research focus

- Artificial intelligence and machine learning in neurodevelopmental disorders (ADHD, autism)
- AI-supported simulation in medical education: virtual patients and parents for interview training and OSCE assessment
- Trauma, PTSD and EMDR, including HPA-axis biomarkers
- Inflammatory and neuroendocrine biomarkers in mood and tic disorders
- Early childhood and parent–child interaction

## Current projects

- **[InsightADHD](https://miracbarisusta.com/insightadhd/)** *(in development)*: an AI-supported, automated neuropsychological test battery for ADHD assessment. Founder; leading clinical design and validation.
- **Virtual parent interview simulation** *(pilot)*: AI-supported virtual parents and patients for training residents and medical students in clinical interviewing, and for OSCE assessment.
